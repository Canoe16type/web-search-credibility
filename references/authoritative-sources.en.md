# Authoritative Sources and Query Patterns

A supplement to `SKILL.en.md`. It is not tied to any country, platform, or search vendor.

## General priority

1. Official text / standard / first-party announcement
2. Official repository, release, changelog
3. Official help center or knowledge base
4. Signed, edited, reliable secondary source
5. Community-maintained material (label as unofficial)

## By domain

### Software and APIs

Official docs (`docs.<domain>`, `<domain>/docs`); official repositories (README, Releases, CHANGELOG, Issues); package registries (npm, PyPI, Maven Central, crates.io); standards (W3C, IETF RFC, ISO/IEC, ECMA).

```text
<product> official docs <topic>
<product> release notes <version>
<repo> GitHub releases <version>
<error code> official documentation
```

### Policy, law, and public services

Government or regulator sites; laws, standards, and policy texts; official announcements and disclosures; international organizations by topic.

```text
<policy name> official site
<law/regulation> full text
<agency> announcement <date>
```

Do not treat one country's domain list as a global rule; use the official sites of the relevant jurisdiction.

### Companies and finance

Company site and investor relations; exchange announcements and regulatory filings; company registries by region; annual reports, prospectuses, financial statements.

```text
<company> investor relations annual report
<company> official announcement <event>
<ticker> exchange filing
```

### Academia and medicine

DOI, PubMed, arXiv, journal sites; universities, research institutes, health agencies; systematic reviews, guidelines, clinical trial registries.

```text
<topic> systematic review
<topic> site:pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov
<guideline> official
```

### Games, anime, and fiction

Official sites, official wikis, official announcements; official social/news pages; community wikis only as supplements, clearly labeled unofficial; numbers, versions, prices, and drop rates from official sources or actual testing.

```text
<work> official wiki <term>
<work> official announcement <update>
<work> <term> wiki
```

Multilingual: confirm the name in the user's language, then verify against the original language or English.

### Products and pricing

Official store or site pricing; official specification pages; marketplace pages only as leads, verify with official sources.

## Judging source quality

Prefer: named author/institution, publication date, references to primary sources, original text, agreement across independent sources.

Be wary of: unsigned aggregators, clickbait, content farms, stale pages, conclusions without evidence.
